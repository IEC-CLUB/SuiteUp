Outsource

🤝

Hiring an external partner to handle a portion of the workload instead of building an internal, in-house team.

Where to Outsource?

📣
Marketing: Hiring an external ad agency.
🧮
Finance: Using outside accountants or auditors.
💻
Tech: Hiring an external developer team.
🚚
Op&Log: Using a 3PL (Third-Party Logistics) provider.

When to Outsource?

⏰

Your current team is heavily limited on time.

⭐

Your current team lacks the specific, specialized expertise needed for the task.

Tradeoffs

1

High ongoing service and rental fees.

2

Your business becomes dependent on an outside partner's performance.

Facility Upgrade

🏢

Investing money to improve your company's existing infrastructure, equipment, and operating systems.

Where to Upgrade?

📣
Marketing: Building an in-house production studio.
🧮
Finance: Implementing an internal ERP software system.
💻
Tech: Purchasing your own servers and IT infrastructure.
🚚
Op&Log: Buying your own warehouses and delivery trucks.

When to Upgrade?

⏰

Your daily operations are completely stable, and you are actively preparing to expand.

🎯

You are making a long-term investment to increase overall operational efficiency.

Tradeoffs

1

Massive upfront investment costs are required.

2

High financial risk if the business attempts to scale in the wrong direction.

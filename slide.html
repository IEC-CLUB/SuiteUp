Strategy 01

Outsource

Hiring an external partner to handle a portion of the workload instead of building an internal, in-house team.

🤝
01
📍

Where to Outsource?

📣
MarketingHiring an external ad agency.
🧮
FinanceUsing outside accountants or auditors.
💻
TechHiring an external developer team.
🚚
Op & LogUsing a 3PL (Third-Party Logistics) provider.
02
⏱️

When to Outsource?

⏰
Time ConstraintsYour current team is heavily limited on time.
⭐
Lack of ExpertiseYour team lacks the specific, specialized expertise needed for the task.
03
⚖️

Tradeoffs

1
Ongoing CostsHigh ongoing service and rental fees.
2
Dependency RiskYour business becomes dependent on an outside partner's performance.
Strategy 02

Facility Upgrade

Investing money to improve your company's existing infrastructure, equipment, and operating systems.

🏢
01
📍

Where to Upgrade?

📣
MarketingBuilding an in-house production studio.
🧮
FinanceImplementing an internal ERP software system.
💻
TechPurchasing your own servers and IT infrastructure.
🚚
Op & LogBuying your own warehouses and delivery trucks.
02
🎯

When to Upgrade?

📈
Preparation for ScaleDaily operations are stable, and you are actively preparing to expand.
🛠️
Long-Term InvestmentMaking a long-term investment to increase overall operational efficiency.
03
⚠️

Tradeoffs

1
Upfront CapitalMassive upfront investment costs are required.
2
Financial RiskHigh financial risk if the business attempts to scale in the wrong direction.
